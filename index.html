Case study · 0 to 1

Pupmeow

Delhi NCR's pet community, for dog and cat families.

ProductCommunityAIPWA
Visit pupmeow.in GitHub
The problem

Pet owners in Delhi NCR relied on scattered WhatsApp groups for lost pets, paid for "adoptions", and kept vaccine records on paper.

The bet

One neighbourhood app that does the few things owners need most, free forever, across six NCR cities.

What I shipped
  • Lost and found with a printable QR poster and "I saw him" sighting alerts
  • Free adoption listings for fosters and NGOs, credited by name
  • Playdates where WhatsApp opens only when both owners agree
  • Pet profiles with vaccine reminders sent to the phone calendar
  • Kaia, an AI helper for everyday care questions that points to a vet for anything serious
Decisions

No animal sales. Numbers visible only to signed-in members. Google sign-in, so no passwords to forget.

Proof

33 unit tests and load tests up to 1,000 concurrent requests, finding the scaling bottleneck early.

The problem

Researching an Indian stock meant a quote site, a screener, a news feed and a chatbot that makes things up, each in a different tab.

The bet

One workspace where live data feeds the AI, so the analysis is grounded in numbers you can see, not vibes.

What I shipped
  • Company pages with live quote, metrics, quarterly results and an AI read with strengths, risks, outlook and a risk score
  • Compare up to four companies on a rebased chart, and a screener across sector, market cap, PE and ROE
  • AI chat that receives live quotes and ratios for any stock you mention
  • Portfolio and watchlist with P&L, sector allocation and NSE plus US holdings in rupees
  • News with sentiment, mutual funds, a SIP calculator, a ⌘K command palette and a phone layout
Decisions

No buy or sell calls, no price targets, no guaranteed-return language. Risky phrasing is flagged, and every answer carries a disclaimer.

Trust loop

Each analysis shows the figures it was based on. Thumbs up or down with reasons feed a quality dashboard, backed by a written AI eval.

Under the hood

A market-data layer with fallback endpoints and a 24-hour stale cache, so it degrades gracefully and never invents numbers. JWT auth with guest accounts, rate limiting, route-level code splitting, and CI on every push.
